# AI Safe – safe from AI

**AI Safe** is an Android app: a safe place on your phone to manage documents, photos and videos, protected from AI.
Manage means more than storing: you store, sort, view, edit, share and back up everything inside the safe.

**Why it is useful:** your gallery, cloud backup, keyboard and screen assistants are read by AI more and more.
Anything stored there unencrypted can be read, analysed or used to train AI. AI Safe takes your files out of there:
everything is encrypted (AES-256), the app has no internet access and no account, files stay out of the gallery,
and screenshots of the safe stay black. The app itself contains no AI.

👉 More information, installation and FAQ: **<https://gossevledder.github.io/AI-Safe/>**
· Download: [latest release](https://github.com/GosseVledder/AI-Safe/releases/latest)
(Android 8.0 or newer, 64-bit ARM; NL, EN, DE, FR, IT, ES)

## All features

### Unlocking and security
- PIN (6–16 digits) or pattern, with a delay after wrong attempts
- Fingerprint unlock
- Safe password and *Forgot your PIN?*
- Auto-lock: leaving the app, screen off, inactivity
- Always on: AES-256, keyboard doesn't learn, no internet, no account
- Screenshots and screen recordings of the safe blocked (default)
- Allow screenshots of the safe (off by default)
- Location and identifying data removed when sharing (on by default)
- Your own colour for the safe
- *Since your last visit*: last opening and wrong PIN attempts (can be switched off)

### Adding and capturing
- Photos and videos via the photo picker, no limit
- Documents: PDF, Word, OpenDocument, RTF, Markdown, HTML, text
- Import a whole gallery folder, with subfolders
- Share to the safe from other apps
- Remove originals + reminder about the cloud copy
- Camera straight into the safe, with grid lines and self-timer
- Open an encrypted file from someone else
- Document scanner: edges, perspective, black & white, PDF
- Record voice memos and add audio files

### Organising and finding
- Folders, subfolders, cover, icon and colour per folder
- Search by name in the whole safe
- Filter on photos, videos, documents or audio
- Sorting (also by date added), remembered per folder, a heading per month
- Pin folders to the top
- Drag-select, move, copy, rename
- Undo after moving or deleting
- Favourites, recycle bin, grid 2–24 per row or list
- Take files out of the safe again
- Statistics: content, playing time, storage, backup status
- Safe on the SD card, move the safe

### Viewing and playing
- Open photos, videos, audio and documents inside the app
- Zoom; swipe for next, down to close, up for info
- Video player: seek, ±10 s, mute, full screen, repeat, resume
- Swipe brightness (left) and volume (right) on videos
- Open with another app (after a warning)
- Speed 0.1×–2.5×, default speed, speed remembered per video
- Frame by frame, hold last frame, save a frame as a photo
- Audio continues with the next file
- Animated GIFs and WebP animations

### Slideshow
- Folder, favourites or selection; photos, videos or mixed
- Time per photo, shuffle, subfolders, countdown bar
- All 20 transitions and the transition duration
- Motion (Ken Burns), calm or strong
- Music from the safe
- Caption with date (and name)

### Editing
- Rename, choose a folder cover
- Redact photos and PDFs: make things truly unreadable
- Photo as wallpaper (after a warning)
- Crop and rotate photos, trim videos
- Adjust photos: brightness, contrast, saturation, filters
- Redact other documents into a PDF

### Sharing
- Share to an app without a copy on your phone (after a warning)
- Receive and open an encrypted share file
- Encrypted share: create a share file yourself
- Show and scan the passphrase as a QR code
- Print photos and PDFs

### Backup and app
- Encrypted backup with its own password, reminder, restore
- Check a backup without restoring it
- Theme, 6 languages, help and FAQ, check for updates

## Limits
AI Safe cannot protect copies that are already in a cloud backup, nor files you take out of the safe or share
yourself. Read the [disclaimer](https://gossevledder.github.io/AI-Safe/#disclaimer) before you rely on the app.
